Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All portfolio data is stored locally on your device. BTAPEX does not upload any information to external servers.

How do I back up my portfolio?

You can export your full collection to a CSV file from the Settings screen. We also recommend enabling iCloud device backup or iTunes/Finder sync through your iOS device settings.

How are risk scores calculated?

Risk scores combine asset category volatility, liquidity, market depth, provenance strength, and authentication certainty using weighted models documented in our disclaimer.

Can I import data from another app?

Yes — you can import any CSV file that follows the BTAPEX column structure. A template is available from the import screen in Settings.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstalling BTAPEX permanently removes all locally stored portfolio data and photos from your device.

Does BTAPEX provide financial advice?

No. BTAPEX is an organizational and analytical tool. All ROI and monetization simulations are for informational purposes only and do not constitute investment advice.
