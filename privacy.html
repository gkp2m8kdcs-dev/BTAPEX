Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

BTAPEX ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our patrimony and collection management application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

BTAPEX is designed with privacy as a core principle. All data you enter into the App — including asset records, photographs, valuation notes, acquisition history, and risk assessments — is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or sync this data to any external server operated by us.

2. Information We May Collect

The only limited information that may be collected automatically by your device platform includes:

  • Crash logs and anonymous diagnostic data, if you have opted in via your operating system settings.
  • App Store download and update metadata, managed by Apple.

BTAPEX does not maintain user accounts and does not collect personally identifying information such as your name, email address, or location.

3. Photos and Media

Photographs you attach to assets are stored in your device's local storage or in the App's sandboxed container. They are never uploaded to any remote server. If you choose to delete an asset, its associated media is also deleted from your device.

4. Analytics

BTAPEX does not embed tracking SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics tools. Any analytics you see within the App are computed locally from your own data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use your device's built-in photo picker (PhotoKit) to let you select images. This is governed by Apple's privacy framework and your device permissions. We do not share any content with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

BTAPEX is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data Security

Because all data resides on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using device passcodes, biometric authentication, and full-disk encryption (standard on modern iOS devices) to protect your information.

8. Your Rights

You may at any time export your portfolio as a CSV file from within the App, or delete all data by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored records and media.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of BTAPEX after a change indicates your acceptance of the updated Policy.
